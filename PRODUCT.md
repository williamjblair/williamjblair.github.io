# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers, collaborators, and curious visitors who want a concise introduction to William Blair and a direct path to his essays.

## Product Purpose

Present William in his own voice and make his writing easy to discover. Success means a first-time visitor quickly understands what he works on and can choose an essay to read.

## Positioning

A personal homepage that behaves like the opening leaf of a scientific or literary essay rather than a conventional portfolio, résumé, or marketing site.

## Operating Context

The homepage is a quiet, vertically scrolling reading surface. Visitors arrive from links, scan a short biography, and continue to a compact essay list.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React and Vite in the existing repository, deployed as a static Vercel-compatible build.
- Two external essay links are live, alongside a local blank-slate route for “Constellations of Borrowed Light.”
- “Constellations of Borrowed Light” opens to a blank cream page until its essay content is ready.
- No navigation, cards, calls to action, portfolio grids, or application-style chrome.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name William Blair and the first-person “Will.” The confirmed voice is thoughtful, direct, literary, scientifically curious, and free of promotional language. The Vela “Constellations” page is a binding craft reference without being a template to copy.

## Evidence on Hand

The approved biography, essay titles, and two external URLs are supplied in the homepage brief. No testimonials, client claims, résumé facts, or additional publications should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Let writing and typographic hierarchy lead.
- Make the path from introduction to essays immediate and unambiguous.
- Prefer restraint, specificity, and negative space over portfolio conventions.
- Keep the page accessible, fast, and fully legible without motion or decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings and links, visible keyboard focus, strong contrast, responsive text, reduced-motion support, and decoration that remains hidden from assistive technology.
